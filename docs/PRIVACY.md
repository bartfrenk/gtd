# Privacy Policy

`gtd` runs locally on your machine. It does not collect, transmit, or store
any data on servers operated by the authors.

- Configuration (including API credentials) lives in your local
  `config.yaml` and never leaves your machine except to talk directly to
  the third-party services you configure (e.g. Google, Spotify), using your
  own credentials.
- No telemetry, analytics, or usage tracking is included.
- Data handled by those third-party services is subject to their own
  privacy policies.
